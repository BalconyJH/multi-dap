# Code of Conduct

multi-dap participants must help maintain a professional, inclusive, and
technically honest project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good faith while evaluating claims from
  evidence.
- Welcome questions and constructive disagreement without personal attacks.
- Give credit, disclose conflicts of interest, and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private, proprietary, security-sensitive, and hardware-identifying
  information.
- Keep synthetic, software-only, and hardware-observed results clearly
  distinguished.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, and knowingly misleading technical or evidence claims are
unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, limit
participation, or ban participants when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an
available verified GitHub channel. Do not place sensitive personal details in
a public issue. A maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves;
another maintainer should review it confidentially and proportionally.
